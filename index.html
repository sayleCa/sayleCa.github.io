Probability · Combinatorics · Graph Theory

My research interests lie at the intersection of probability and combinatorics, with applications to graph theory. I am particularly interested in free probability and non-commutative probability, as well as limit theorems, random matrices, set partitions, cumulants, spectral graph theory, graph energy, and random graphs.
