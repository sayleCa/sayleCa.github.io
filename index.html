Saylé

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at the Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla (BUAP), Mexico, where I have been pursuing my doctoral studies since 2023 under the supervision of Octavio Arizmendi Echegaray and Hugo Adán Cruz Suárez. I expect to receive my Ph.D. in December 2026.

I obtained my Master of Science in Mathematics from BUAP in 2023, graduating magna cum laude. Previously, I obtained my Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from BUAP in 2020.

My research interests lie at the intersection of probability and combinatorics, with applications to graph theory. I am particularly interested in free probability and non-commutative probability, as well as limit theorems, random matrices, set partitions, cumulants, spectral graph theory, graph energy, and random graphs.
