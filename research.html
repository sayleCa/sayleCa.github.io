Research

My research lies at the intersection of probability and combinatorics, with applications to graph theory.

01

Free Probability & Non-Commutative Probability

Free probability, second-order phenomena, free cumulants, fluctuations of non-commutative random variables.

02

Probability & Random Matrices

Limit theorems, random matrix models, fluctuations, and probabilistic methods for asymptotic problems.

03

Combinatorics

Set partitions, cumulants, generating functions, non-crossing objects, and combinatorial techniques connected to probability.

04

Graph Theory

Spectral graph theory, graph energy, random graphs, and structural properties of graph and network families.
